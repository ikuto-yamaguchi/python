# Intelligence roadmap and architecture stop rules

## Diagnosis

The project has accumulated real local mechanisms, but recent CAP-SEM work stayed inside a controlled relation-graph family. Passing additional variants in the same algebra does not provide a credible forecast to Japanese high-school intelligence or strong-LLM parity.

The public frozen evidence is substantially weaker than the controlled gates:

- Phase 13a: 40/200 overall, with 0% on four of five non-direct-arithmetic axes.
- Phase 15a: 0/200 on five newly frozen public axes.
- Phase 16a: 0/200 on another five newly frozen public axes.

Therefore the current architecture is not on a demonstrated monotonic path to broad intelligence. CAP-SEM-001 through CAP-SEM-005 remain useful component evidence, but they no longer define the critical path.

## Critical-path change

Further semantic micro-gates are frozen. The critical path is now:

1. one checkpoint;
2. one learning rule and runtime;
3. no benchmark-name routing;
4. simultaneous evaluation across unrelated public and newly authored domains;
5. aggregate score and minimum-domain score reported together;
6. architecture changes accepted only when several domains improve without material regression.

A new experiment is admitted to the critical path only when it declares in advance which integrated public or prospectively frozen scores it should improve. Synthetic-only success without an immediate external transfer test remains component research, not main progress.

## Executable milestones

### CAP-GEN-001 — integrated public reality baseline

Run one frozen worker and fingerprint on all 600 examples from the three prior public slices: 15 axes, 40 examples per axis. Task and axis names are hidden from the worker and retained only by the scorer.

This gate establishes the actual baseline. It does not require a high score to be protocol-valid.

Development progress is measured by:

- overall accuracy;
- minimum axis accuracy;
- number of axes above 20%, 50%, and 70%;
- coverage and selective accuracy;
- model, knowledge, memory, training, and inference resources.

### CAP-GEN-002 — unified cross-domain acquisition

The same learned artifact must improve the 15-axis suite without adding an axis-specific handler, parser, prompt template, document, or routing branch.

Promotion gate:

- at least 50% aggregate accuracy;
- at least 20% on every axis;
- at least 40% on five prospectively frozen axes not used during development;
- no previously measured axis loses more than two percentage points;
- executable total description and all induction/inference compute reported.

The intended architectural target is a trainable shared latent sequence/event model plus a generic program/runtime library. A larger list of hand-authored symbolic machines is not sufficient.

### CAP-GEN-003 — natural Japanese, mathematics, science, and code integration

One checkpoint must jointly handle:

- Japanese expository and narrative reading;
- multi-step arithmetic and high-school algebra;
- physics/chemistry/biology rule learning and experiment interpretation;
- code behavior prediction and small failing-test diagnosis;
- short lessons defining unfamiliar notation and laws.

Promotion gate:

- at least 60% aggregate;
- at least 40% in every domain;
- at least 50% on prospectively frozen cross-domain compositions;
- no task-family-specific solver additions;
- tool-free and tool-assisted scores reported separately.

### HSS-001 — strong Japanese high-school-student gate

The existing frozen target remains:

- at least 80% aggregate;
- at least 70% in every required curriculum domain;
- at least 75% on newly authored or procedurally generated unseen items;
- at least 70% on rubric-graded written responses;
- at least 80% correction of deliberately flawed solutions;
- practical repository work, tables, and multi-page Japanese documents included.

Passing CAP-GEN-001 through CAP-GEN-003 is necessary but not sufficient for HSS-001.

### LLM-PARITY-001 — matched strong-model parity

A parity claim additionally requires:

- a frozen broad suite covering generation, dialogue, knowledge, reasoning, coding, long context, and tool use;
- matched prompts, demonstrations, tools, and stopping rules;
- comparison with a current strong open or hosted model;
- non-inferior quality across every major family, not only aggregate score;
- complete lifetime resource accounting.

## Honest forecast

There is no defensible calendar date for HSS-001 or LLM-PARITY-001. They are open research outcomes, not scheduled engineering deliverables.

The defensible forecast is conditional:

- Under the previous CAP-SEM-only trajectory: no credible route; practical forecast is never.
- Under the new integrated trajectory: HSS candidacy begins only after CAP-GEN-002 and CAP-GEN-003 pass. It is not one or two micro-capabilities away.
- Strong-LLM parity is strictly later and may require an architecture qualitatively different from the current symbolic system.

Calendar planning is replaced by evidence checkpoints. A date estimate becomes defensible only after at least two consecutive major architecture iterations improve both aggregate accuracy and the minimum-axis floor on the frozen integrated gate. Before that evidence exists, any month or year would be invented.

## Mandatory stop and pivot rules

The project must not preserve an architecture merely because local gates pass.

1. **Three-cycle score rule**: after three major architecture iterations, if CAP-GEN-001 aggregate accuracy has improved by less than 15 percentage points total, abandon the current core architecture.
2. **Zero-floor rule**: if any public axis remains at 0% after two major iterations, the next iteration must change the shared representation or learning objective, not add another local solver.
3. **Transfer rule**: an improvement that affects only the development axes and scores below 40% on prospectively frozen axes is rejected.
4. **Complexity rule**: if score gains require roughly linear growth in handlers or task descriptions, the mechanism is not general learning and is rejected.
5. **Regression rule**: gains that reduce a previously passed domain by more than two points require rollback or a documented Pareto justification.
6. **Synthetic saturation rule**: no new synthetic-only gate may enter the critical path unless it predicts a frozen public improvement and that prediction is tested immediately.

## Next architectural experiment

After CAP-GEN-001 fixes the baseline, the next experiment must train one shared latent model on mixed raw streams rather than extend the relation ontology. Candidate mechanisms must jointly support:

- byte/subword sequence prediction;
- event and variable binding;
- reusable latent operations;
- externalized episodic and semantic memory;
- program execution and verification;
- online library growth with minimum-description regularization.

The candidate is judged first by multi-axis transfer, not by whether its internal representation resembles an LLM or the current graph runtime.
