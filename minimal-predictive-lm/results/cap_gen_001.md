## Diagnosis

The current system is highly accurate when one of its existing symbolic machines covers the prompt, but it does not transfer to five new semantic families. The result is a bimodal capability profile rather than a gradually generalizing learner.

Further CAP-SEM micro-gates are frozen from the critical path. The next architecture must move several of the five zero axes with one shared representation and learning objective. Adding separate causal, reference, validity, adjective, or belief handlers does not count.

## Decision

- CAP-GEN-001 establishes a valid integrated baseline but does not pass its capability threshold.
- CAP-GEN-002 starts from **66.3% aggregate / 0.0% floor**.
- Two major iterations with any axis still at 0% force a shared-representation or objective change.
- Three major iterations with less than fifteen aggregate percentage points of total improvement force abandonment of the current core architecture.
- A Japanese high-school or general-LLM claim remains forbidden.

## Claim boundary

This fifteen-axis public reasoning proxy is necessary but not sufficient for Japanese high-school intelligence. Japanese curriculum coverage, science, humanities, writing, repository coding, long context, generation, dialogue, and interactive learning remain separate required gates.
