## Family management

### Frozen: HF-013

Root premise rejected:

> A semantic unit has one global surface segmentation/order/template, and examples inconsistent with that form should be eliminated before semantic identity is established.

This premise failed across B, C and D. The same latent causal structure must tolerate episode-local order, omission, extra words and discourse realization until external outcomes force a shared structural core.

### Active priority: AF-011

Surface realizations remain nuisance alternatives. Identity is attributed only to the minimal structural core—segmentation/factorization/arity/argument link/causal mapping—that independently reconverges under disjoint witness sets and supports held-out behavior.

## Maximum upstream bottleneck

Remove the remaining object/operation character-class, operation-family and arity-multiset oracles without reintroducing hypothesis-space destruction. The system must preserve episode-local surface alternatives while independently converging on the same raw structural core, then show an external capability advantage over Random and all shuffles.
