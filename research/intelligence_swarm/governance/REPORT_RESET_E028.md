## 1. Prior-art and official-code boundary

The current accepted boundary remains C020. Li, Kaba and Ravanbakhsh (AISTATS 2025) characterise the maximal causal abstraction identifiable from paired observations under unknown subset interventions as an intervention-induced quotient. Language that only names, predicts or reconstructs that quotient does not strictly identify a finer latent intervention-target partition.

The surviving RQ is therefore limited to strict refinement of a residual quotient block by a population language channel with externally fixed denotational anchors. It remains unadopted. No architecture implementation is authorised before public-baseline reproduction and preregistration.
