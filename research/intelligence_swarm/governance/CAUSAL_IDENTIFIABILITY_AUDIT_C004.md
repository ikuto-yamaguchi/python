# Causal Identifiability Audit C004

Date: 2026-07-25

## Scope

This cycle does not introduce a model or mechanism. It audits whether RQ-001-N3 is operationally identifiable on the currently pinned public benchmark.

## Finding 1 — SILG/RTFM can test Gate L, not Gate I

The pinned RTFM S1 interface exposes:

- symbolic text fields (`wiki`, `task`, `inv`),
- a 6×6 named grid and relative/absolute position,
- a valid-action mask,
- five navigation actions (`stay`, `up`, `down`, `left`, `right`),
- train/test environment IDs and episode outcomes.

It does **not** expose or define:

- a family of latent mechanism interventions,
- intervention-target labels or target subsets,
- pre/post pairs generated by a declared intervention operator,
- a ground-truth causal abstraction or latent partition,
- an equivalence relation between utterances and mechanism interventions.

Therefore RTFM supports a matched test of whether language improves action/task prediction beyond state/history controls (**Gate L**), but it cannot by itself score recovery of an utterance-conditioned latent intervention partition (**Gate I**).

Any Gate-I score added to RTFM without an externally specified intervention family would be created by the researcher and would reintroduce a hand-designed ontology. That is prohibited by the reconstruction policy.

## Finding 2 — adjacent prior art further narrows the claim

Two additional 2025 primary results reduce the defensible novelty space:

1. *Towards the Causal Complete Cause of Multi-Modal Representation Learning* defines causal sufficiency and necessity for multimodal representations and gives an identification/measurement framework using an instrumental variable and counterfactual branch. Thus a generic claim that language/world representations should be causally sufficient and necessary is not novel.
2. *Learning Causal Response Representations through Direct Effect Analysis* learns outcome directions most directly caused by an observed treatment and provides statistical guarantees. Thus treatment-conditioned response representation is also prior art.

Neither result jointly induces raw-language equivalence classes and unknown latent intervention partitions from ungrouped interactive trajectories. They are adjacent rather than exact overlap, but they prevent broad causal-sufficiency or causal-response claims from serving as the contribution.

## Decision

### RQ-001-N3 as a single-benchmark joint claim

**REJECTED ON SILG/RTFM.**

Reason: Gate I is not operationally defined by the benchmark observation/action schema. A learner cannot be judged against a latent intervention partition that the benchmark does not specify or generate.

### Gate L on SILG/RTFM

**RETAINED AS A REPRODUCTION AND NECESSITY TEST, NOT A NOVELTY CLAIM.**

Required matched controls remain:

- official recurrent,
- random valid action,
- language-blind,
- state/history-only,
- environment-ID-only,
- within-environment language shuffle,
- transition/outcome shuffle.

A positive result only establishes language-specific predictive utility on the benchmark. It does not establish semantic or causal identifiability.

### Gate I

**DEFERRED TO BENCHMARK SELECTION.**

Before any architecture is allowed, the program must identify a public dataset/environment that supplies all of the following without researcher-authored semantic slots:

1. interactive or longitudinal trajectories;
2. observed raw language associated with episodes or interventions;
3. explicit pre/post mechanism-change data;
4. a ground-truth intervention family or a formally justified abstraction target;
5. held-out targets/mechanisms and permutation-aware evaluation;
6. enough intervention diversity to separate at least two nontrivial latent partitions.

If no such public benchmark exists after a bounded primary-source search, the joint empirical RQ is rejected and only a theoretical impossibility/identifiability result may remain.

## Updated research decomposition

- **R0.1 / R0.2:** reproduce Gate-L baselines on SILG/RTFM.
- **R0.3a:** benchmark audit for a valid Gate-I dataset; no model training.
- **R0.3b:** only after a benchmark passes the audit, preregister target-known / target-set-known / target-unknown / label-shuffle comparisons.
- **R0.4:** J-CRe3 remains a Japanese reference-grounding realism audit and does not supply Gate-I causal interventions.

## Falsification and stop conditions

Reject the remaining joint empirical program if:

- no public benchmark satisfies the Gate-I requirements;
- language gains disappear under state/history/environment controls;
- the only intervention partition is researcher-authored;
- recovery is evaluated by exact names rather than joint permutation or supported abstraction;
- intervention diversity cannot separate competing partitions.
