## 1. Primary literature and official-code overlap audit

C032 audited Li, Kaba, and Ravanbakhsh, **On the Identifiability of Causal Abstractions** (AISTATS 2025 / arXiv:2503.10834).

Under paired pre/post observations, random unknown perfect interventions on arbitrary latent subsets, faithfulness, regular mechanisms/noise, and an invertible smooth observation map, the intervention family can identify a maximal quotient causal abstraction. Its blocks are induced by the sigma-algebra generated by non-descendant sets of the available intervention targets. Therefore non-atomic unknown interventions do not imply total non-identifiability, and discovering intervention-induced blocks is not a language-specific contribution.

No paper-specific immutable official repository with dependency lock and reproduction commands was verified. No public baseline experiment was started in C032.

RQ-001 is narrowed to distinctions strictly inside a residual quotient block after the strongest non-language abstraction has been computed. Language must contain information unavailable from paired observations, actions, outcomes, environment identity, completed trajectories, and quotient signatures, and must eliminate every within-block joint recoding of target members, utterance classes, denotation, and encoder/decoder.

Decision:

> **NARROWED BEYOND INTERVENTION-INDUCED CAUSAL ABSTRACTION IDENTIFIABILITY — NOT ADOPTED**
