## Primary work newly audited

### On the Identifiability of Causal Abstractions

Xiusi Li, Sékou-Oumar Kaba, and Siamak Ravanbakhsh. AISTATS 2025 / arXiv:2503.10834.

Primary records:

- PMLR: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/li25g.html
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10834
- OpenReview: https://openreview.net/forum?id=RKiOGRrABL

The paper studies weakly supervised causal representation learning from observable pre/post pairs generated by random unknown perfect interventions on arbitrary subsets of latent variables. The intervention target is not observed. The observation map is assumed invertible and smooth, and the latent SCM is assumed faithful with absolutely continuous, differentiable mechanisms and noise.

The main result does not require atomic interventions on every latent variable. Instead, Theorem 3.1 identifies the latent SCM up to a quotient abstraction whose blocks are determined by the sigma-algebra generated by the non-descendant sets of the available intervention targets:

\[
G_{abs}=G^\star / \mathcal P\!\left(\sigma(\operatorname{nd}(\mathcal I^\star))\right).
\]

Theorem 3.2 can identify additional latent subspaces when the intersection of intervention targets sharing a non-descendant set is a singleton, but it then drops constraints on the causal mechanisms between the additionally separated variables.

The authors explicitly distinguish population identifiability from learning: their appendix contains a small linear-Gaussian illustration, but the paper does not claim a scalable estimator for recovering the abstract causal model. The inspected PMLR, arXiv, OpenReview, author, and targeted GitHub records did not expose a paper-specific official repository with an immutable commit, dependency lock, and reproduction commands. Therefore this cycle did not start a public baseline experiment.

## Theorem / assumption boundary added to the prior-art matrix

This result removes another broad novelty premise from RQ-001: arbitrary subset interventions do not imply that the only scientifically valid outcome is either full latent recovery or total non-identifiability.

Under paired pre/post observations, unknown perfect subset interventions, faithfulness, absolute continuity, differentiable mechanisms, and a diffeomorphic mixing map, the intervention family itself induces a maximal recoverable causal abstraction. The identifiable object is a quotient graph and corresponding latent blocks determined by which variables remain non-descendants across intervention targets.

The following claims are therefore excluded from the novelty space:

- non-atomic unknown interventions require language before any causal structure can be recovered;
- failure to recover individual latent variables means no causal representation is identifiable;
- discovering intervention-induced latent blocks is itself a new language-grounding principle;
- naming the quotient blocks with natural language refines the causal abstraction;
- task success, next-state prediction, or language-shuffle gaps demonstrate recovery inside an abstraction block;
- an arbitrary fine-grained target partition is the scientifically correct ground truth when the intervention family only identifies a coarser quotient.

The paper also sharpens the benchmark contract. Before asking whether language identifies a finer target partition, RQ-001 must compute the strongest abstraction already implied by the non-language intervention family. Any proposed language contribution must operate strictly inside a block that remains unresolved after this quotient construction.

## Identifiability counterexample: correct quotient recovery without internal partition recovery

Let the strongest non-language paired-intervention statistic identify the quotient partition

\[
\mathcal B=\{B_1,\ldots,B_m\}=\mathcal P\!\left(\sigma(\operatorname{nd}(\mathcal I))\right).
\]

Assume one block contains two latent targets, \(B_j=\{t_1,t_2\}\), because every available intervention gives \(t_1\) and \(t_2\) the same non-descendant signature. The paired observation distribution therefore identifies \(B_j\) as a block but does not distinguish its internal members.

Now add raw utterances generated only from the identified block and observable history:

\[
U\sim p(U\mid B_j,X,\widetilde X,A,Y,H).
\]

Construct two candidate models:

- Model A uses an internal target partition \(P=\{\{t_1\},\{t_2\}\}\), utterance equivalence \(Q\), and denotation map \(d:U/Q\to P\).
- Model B applies a nontrivial permutation or smooth reparameterization inside \(B_j\), changes the internal partition to \(P'\), recodes the utterance classes to \(Q'\), and transforms the language encoder/generator so the raw-utterance distribution is unchanged.

Both models preserve:

- the full pre/post observable-pair distribution;
- the intervention-family non-descendant signatures;
- the identifiable quotient graph and every quotient block;
- all high-level interventional queries expressible on the quotient;
- next-state prediction, action accuracy, and task success for policies using only quotient-level information;
- utterance likelihood, paraphrase accuracy, and language-shuffle gaps after joint recoding.

Nevertheless, \(P\neq P'\) and \(Q\neq Q'\). Correct recovery of the maximal intervention-induced causal abstraction therefore does not identify the internal target partition or raw-language equivalence.

This also blocks a common false-positive interpretation: if language improves finite-sample learning of the already identifiable quotient, that is an estimation improvement, not evidence that language breaks the within-block symmetry.

## Necessary boundary for a surviving language contribution

Let \(S_{abs}\) denote the complete quotient graph, quotient partition, non-descendant signatures, paired observations, actions, outcomes, and interaction history available without language. Let \(P_{residual}\) be a proposed partition strictly finer than the identifiable quotient.

A necessary information condition is

\[
I(P_{residual};L\mid S_{abs})>0.
\]

This is still insufficient. The language law must also be externally fixed before fitting and must prohibit every automorphism that acts inside a quotient block while jointly recoding:

- residual target members;
- raw-utterance equivalence classes;
- the denotation map;
- the language encoder/decoder; and
- any target-name prediction head.

If language only names quotient blocks, paraphrases their observable effects, or encodes an intervention signature already recoverable from the paired trajectory, it cannot refine the population equivalence class.

## Updated decision for RQ-001

**NARROWED BEYOND INTERVENTION-INDUCED CAUSAL ABSTRACTION IDENTIFIABILITY — NOT ADOPTED**

The only remaining candidate is:

> After computing the maximal causal abstraction identifiable from the benchmark's unknown intervention family and complete non-language paired trajectory, can a preregistered externally fixed language law distinguish members inside a residual quotient block, jointly identify raw-utterance equivalence and the finer intervention-target partition, and eliminate every within-block joint automorphism?

Adoption now requires at minimum:

1. an explicit construction of the intervention-family non-descendant signatures for the benchmark;
2. the maximal quotient partition and quotient graph identifiable without language;
3. a proof or executable countermodel showing which within-block distinctions remain unresolved;
4. language information unavailable from paired observations, actions, rewards, outcomes, environment IDs, and completed trajectories;
5. a denotational anchor fixed before fitting;
6. a proof that the within-block joint automorphism group becomes trivial;
7. an impossibility result when language only names or paraphrases an identifiable quotient block;
8. direct recovery metrics for the residual target partition and raw-utterance partition, not only task success or prediction accuracy;
9. quotient-aware language-blind, state-only, target-label-shuffle, outcome-shuffle, and block-label-shuffle controls on identical instances;
10. immutable reproduction of an applicable public non-language baseline before architecture work;
11. preregistration before any new mechanism is authorized.
