## Research question registry

### RQ-001 — Joint language/causal identifiability under unknown intervention targets

Status: **CANDIDATE GAP — NOVELTY NOT ESTABLISHED**

Question:

> Can latent intervention targets and raw-language equivalence classes be jointly identified from interactive trajectories when semantic parsers, object slots, intervention labels, pretrained language models and external ontologies are unavailable?

Adjacent prior art:

- interventional causal representation learning identifies latent causal factors under intervention assumptions
- unknown multi-node intervention work removes known-target assumptions in restricted observation models
- environment-first instruction grounding learns transition representations before language
- incremental grounding learns from action traces
- SILG evaluates multi-domain interactive language grounding
- J-CRe3 evaluates Japanese real-world reference grounding

Open audit:

- whether any existing method jointly identifies latent causal variables and language equivalence classes
- whether unknown intervention targets and raw language create a new identifiability obstruction
- whether a provable condition can be stated without hand-written slots or pretrained encoders

Promotion requirement:

1. systematic prior-art search finds no direct solution
2. one public grounded-language baseline and one causal-representation baseline are reproduced
3. a mathematical claim or preregistered falsifiable hypothesis is written
4. expected difference from the strongest baseline is explicit
5. resource target remains below 1GB at inference
