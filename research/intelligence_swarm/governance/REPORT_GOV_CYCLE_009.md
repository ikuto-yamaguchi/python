## Research-program change

Previous benchmark logic mixed two failures:

1. **Identifiability failure**: multiple hidden semantic mappings produce exactly the same observations.
2. **Representation/learning failure**: the mapping is identifiable from available witnesses, but the learner does not recover or reuse it.

From this cycle onward, these are separated. Budget-zero opaque performance is a symmetry audit only. Capability progress is measured after a minimal, non-oracle calibration witness set.

## Common benchmark v7

Required phases:

1. Initial raw-Japanese structural hypotheses.
2. Witness selection without final-answer access.
3. Calibration outcome and version-space update.
4. Independent disjoint witness-set re-identification.
5. Contradiction/corruption audit.
6. Held-out evaluation.

Formal-condition prohibitions:

- fixed ontology or slot labels;
- domain correspondence dictionary or shared identity;
- oracle token boundaries;
- oracle factorization into target/operation/relation;
- fixed unary arity;
- final test outcome in witness selection, structure generation or ranking.

Required controls:

- random witness;
- boundary shuffle;
- factor shuffle;
- arity shuffle;
- outcome shuffle;
- witness-set shuffle;
- oracle-structure upper bound reported separately.

Required external abilities:

- prospective target and transition prediction;
- inverse query;
- counterfactual composition and repair;
- held-out tokens and Rename;
- unknown word order, subject omission, multiple paragraphs and free Japanese;
- unseen target-operation/relation combinations.

Provisional promotion threshold:

- Correct exceeds every control by at least 0.10;
- all three seeds pass;
- a disjoint witness set reconverges to the same learned segmentation, arity and causal mapping;
- contradictions are quarantined instead of overwriting the learned identity.
