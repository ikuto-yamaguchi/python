# Causal Identifiability Audit C005

Date: 2026-07-25

## Scope

This cycle performs theorem/assumption comparison only. It adds no architecture, toy mechanism, latent slot inventory, graph learner, memory module, RAG component or external-LLM dependency.

The audited candidate question is whether raw-language equivalence classes and a latent intervention partition can be jointly identified from interactive trajectories without target labels, semantic parsers, object slots, pretrained language models or supplied perturbation semantics.

## New primary-source boundary

### Baumgartner et al., CLeaR 2026

`Disentangling Dynamical Systems: Causal Representation Learning Meets Local Sparse Attention` derives a graphical identifiability criterion for recovering system parameters from raw trajectory data up to permutation and diffeomorphism. Its central warning for this program is that global causal structure only lower-bounds what can be identified; local state-dependent causal structure can be necessary for full disentanglement.

Implication: trajectory-only latent system-parameter recovery is already a developed CRL problem. A new contribution cannot be merely `raw trajectories -> disentangled dynamics`.

Primary source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v323/baumgartner26a.html

### Brouillard et al., CLeaR 2026

`Learning a Spatial Partitioning and its Causal Relations from Temporal Data` establishes identifiability for jointly learning a partition of observed variables into latent factors and a causal graph from temporal data under a single-parent decoding structure, then demonstrates the method on climate data.

Implication: jointly learning a partition and causal relations from temporal observations is also prior art. A new contribution cannot be merely `temporal observations -> latent partition + causal graph`.

Primary source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v323/brouillard26a.html

### Benhamza et al., 2026

`Identifiable Multimodal Causal Representation Learning under Partial Latent Sharing` gives component-wise identifiability for multimodal observations generated by nonlinear mixing from partially shared causal latents.

Implication: using a second modality to resolve latent causal factors is no longer an unoccupied high-level claim. Treating language as an auxiliary modality does not by itself establish novelty.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19135

### Orujlu et al., 2026

`Partially Observed Structural Causal Models` formalizes latent contexts that co-determine interaction structure and mechanisms, and explicitly demonstrates non-identifiability when context is latent and context-level interventions are unavailable.

Implication: if utterances merely proxy an unobserved context that changes both graph structure and mechanisms, observational language-trajectory association does not identify an intervention partition. Some context-separating intervention or equivalent anchor is required.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03268

## Theorem/assumption comparison

| Requirement | Trajectory CRL | Temporal partition learning | Multimodal CRL | Candidate raw-language setting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| aligned observations | trajectory windows | common temporal field | aligned modality samples | utterance and trajectory must be episode-aligned |
| source of asymmetry | local causal structures / parameter variation | single-parent decoding and temporal dependence | partially shared latent structure | currently unspecified without a qualified benchmark |
| intervention family supplied | not necessarily explicit, but structural variation is constrained | no language intervention semantics | no utterance-target semantics | target family is hidden and must not be hand-authored |
| identifiable object | parameters up to permutation/diffeomorphism | spatial partition and causal graph | shared/private causal components | at most joint permutation or intervention-supported abstraction |
| raw language | absent | absent | generic second modality, not interactive instruction semantics | required |
| external capability requirement | factor recovery / prediction | partition and graph recovery | component recovery | held-out mechanism prediction and action success are mandatory |

## Revised no-go boundary

Let `X_{0:T}` be a trajectory, `U` a raw utterance, `Z` latent system factors and `C` a latent context or mechanism regime.

If:

1. `U` is only a proxy for `C`,
2. no intervention or observed variation separates the components of `C`, and
3. the joint distribution is invariant under a simultaneous reparameterization of `(Z, C)` and the utterance classes,

then language-conditioned prediction can improve while the intervention partition remains non-identifiable. The improvement may arise from environment, policy phase, reward density or context prediction rather than semantic intervention identity.

Therefore Gate L remains necessary but cannot certify Gate I.

## Candidate question after C005

The broad joint-identification question remains rejected. The only surviving theoretical candidate is:

> On a public benchmark that independently defines mechanism-changing variation, does episode-aligned raw language eliminate equivalence classes that remain under trajectory-only causal representation learning, yielding a strictly finer intervention-supported causal abstraction and improved held-out mechanism prediction under permutation-aware evaluation?

Status: **RQ-001-N4 — narrowed, not adopted.**

This formulation deliberately requires a strict comparison between:

- trajectory-only identifiability/equivalence class;
- language-plus-trajectory identifiability/equivalence class;
- language-shuffled and context-matched controls;
- held-out mechanism external capability.

A generic multimodal improvement is insufficient.

## Adoption conditions

Adopt RQ-001-N4 only if all are satisfied:

1. A public dataset defines genuine mechanism-changing variation independently of researcher-authored semantic slots.
2. Raw language is temporally aligned but intervention-target labels are withheld.
3. The trajectory-only equivalence class is formally or exhaustively characterized.
4. Adding correct language provably or empirically refines that equivalence class.
5. Within-environment language shuffle, context-ID, reward/history and paraphrase controls exclude proxy explanations.
6. The refined abstraction improves prospective held-out-mechanism prediction or online task success in at least two environment families and seeds 1, 7 and 19.
7. Evaluation is joint-permutation or abstraction aware.

## Rejection conditions

Reject the empirical joint-identification program if:

- no public benchmark satisfies conditions 1–3;
- language does not refine the trajectory-only equivalence class;
- gains disappear under context/reward/history controls;
- the setting is subsumed by aligned multimodal CRL after utterances are treated as a generic modality;
- the target partition must be introduced manually.

## Decision

- Prior-art matrix: refined.
- Theorem/assumption comparison: completed.
- New architecture: prohibited and not introduced.
- Gate L: continues only as public capability reproduction.
- Gate I: remains paused pending a qualifying benchmark.
- RQ status: **narrowed to N4, not adopted**.
- Novelty, intelligence principle and capability progress: not established.
- High-school-level intelligence: not achieved.
