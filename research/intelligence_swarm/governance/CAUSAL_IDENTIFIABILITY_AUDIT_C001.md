# Causal Identifiability Audit C001

## Purpose

This audit replaces another toy mechanism cycle. It asks whether the reconstructed candidate question is scientifically well-posed:

> Can latent intervention targets and raw-language equivalence classes be jointly identified from interactive trajectories when semantic parsers, object slots, intervention labels and pretrained language models are not supplied?

No novelty claim is made here.

## Identifiability consequence

The causal-representation literature establishes a strong warning for RQ-001:

1. Unknown intervention targets are not automatically identifiable from arbitrary trajectories.
2. Existing positive theorems require intervention diversity, perfect/hard interventions, distributional environments, genericity/faithfulness, or restrictions on the observation mixing process.
3. A raw-language channel does not remove the ambiguity unless it contributes an independently constrained view of the same latent variables.
4. If utterances can be arbitrarily permuted together with latent variables and policies, language meaning remains identifiable only up to the same permutation orbit.

Therefore the unrestricted wording of RQ-001 is too broad. The scientifically defensible version must state exactly what breaks the joint latent-language permutation symmetry.

## Narrowed candidate question

> Under multiple uncoupled interactive environments, can a model jointly recover (i) a latent intervention partition and (ii) cross-expression language equivalence up to a shared permutation, when the only cross-environment invariants are transition mechanisms and language-conditioned action consequences?

This is narrower in four ways:

- recovery is only required up to a shared permutation;
- multiple environments are mandatory;
- intervention diversity is an explicit assumption rather than a hidden benchmark convenience;
- language is useful only if it predicts selective transition changes beyond state/action-only controls.

## Minimum observable package

A benchmark instance is not eligible for the research claim unless it contains:

1. pre-action observation;
2. raw instruction text;
3. selected action;
4. next observation;
5. environment identity for training split construction, but no target-variable label;
6. repeated mechanisms under renamed entities and paraphrased instructions;
7. at least two intervention environments per latent mechanism candidate;
8. held-out combinations of entity, dynamics and language form.

The final target state or completed trajectory must not be supplied to the predictor.

## Mandatory controls

- state/action-only
- language-only
- language-shuffled across episodes
- transition-shuffled within environment
- environment-ID-only
- random policy
- official SILG recurrent/shared baseline
- language dynamics pretraining baseline

A language-causal claim requires language-conditioned dynamics or task success to exceed all non-language controls on held-out mechanisms, not merely held-out wording.

## Decision

**NARROW, not ADOPT.**

The broad original question is not yet a defensible novelty claim. Existing CRL theory already covers substantial parts of unknown-intervention identifiability, and interactive grounding already covers language-conditioned control and dynamics. The remaining candidate gap is their joint recovery under a shared-permutation formulation and strict state/action-only controls.

No architecture work is authorized by this audit. The next productive step is reproduction of:

1. SILG shared recurrent baseline without pretrained language models;
2. language-blind/state-only controls;
3. Language Dynamics Distillation or a faithful language-conditioned next-state objective;
4. a hidden-intervention-target split added only after baseline reproduction.
