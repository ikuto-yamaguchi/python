# R0.2 Environment-first Cycle 004

## Decision

Continue R0.2 reproduction, but reject the existing public-trajectory comparison as capability evidence.

No new operation/goal mechanism or architecture was introduced. This cycle adds a data-eligibility guard before environment-first and end-to-end results may be interpreted.

## Why the previous trajectory comparison is not eligible

The trajectories exported from workflow run `30109823906` were generated by an undertrained recurrent policy.

Across the three canonical seeds:

- seed 1: 0 successful train episodes out of 40
- seed 7: 0 successful train episodes out of 40; action 0 accounts for 97.42% of all train transitions
- seed 19: 1 successful train episode out of 40
- all three test sets: 0 successful episodes out of 20

Seed 7 also has only one action with at least 5% support. A representation learner can obtain high offline action accuracy by modelling policy collapse or local state regularities rather than instruction-conditioned task dynamics.

Therefore the earlier observations

- environment-first action accuracy 0.6840
- end-to-end 0.6907
- state-only 0.7240
- no change under language removal or language shuffle

remain negative diagnostics only. They do not establish that environment-first pretraining fails in a competent instruction-following regime, nor that language is unnecessary.

## Added guard

`audit_r02_trajectory_eligibility.py` now records, per seed and split:

- successful episode count and rate
- action histogram and entropy
- majority-action share
- number of actions with at least 5% support
- unique language observations
- dataset SHA-256

Default eligibility conditions are:

- majority-action share <= 0.90
- at least 5 successful train episodes per seed
- at least 2 actions with >=5% support

These are minimum anti-collapse checks, not a capability threshold. Passing them does not make the benchmark reproduction complete.

The workflow now runs this audit after trajectory export and stores the result and log in the reproducibility artifact. It does not abort artifact upload when the trajectory is ineligible.

## Next decision

The active 32,768-frame recurrent run must be evaluated first. R0.2 may proceed only if its exported trajectories pass the eligibility guard. If they fail, the next action is to increase or repair the public behavior-policy training budget rather than tune the representation model on failed demonstrations.

Even after eligibility passes, formal R0.2 reproduction still requires:

- online task success
- typed next-state objectives
- matched end-to-end parameter and data budget
- real held-out entity, dynamics and language-form splits
- random, state-only, language-blind, language-shuffle and transition/outcome-shuffle controls
- per-seed model, data and log checksums
